Summary

I work on language technology and data infrastructure for health in Sub-Saharan Africa. I build and evaluate bilingual English–Luganda assistants that answer only from expert-approved or official sources, and open tools that use language models to help research projects harmonise their data.

Experience

2021 – present

Data Science Specialist, eLwazi Open Data Science Platform

Uganda Virus Research Institute, Entebbe · NIH-funded DS-I Africa program

  • Leads the eLwazi Data Models, Storage, Registries and Catalogue Working Group, which has had between about 20 and 50 members from DS-I Africa projects.
  • Co-developer of the Metadata Harmonisation Tool, an open-source application that uses large language models to map study variables to a target codebook.
  • Co-author (ideas and editing) of papers on the institute's bioinformatics computing service (Bioinformatics, 2026; PLOS Computational Biology, 2025).

2019 – present

Senior Data Scientist and ML Technical Lead

Africa Center of Excellence in Bioinformatics and Data-Intensive Sciences (ACE Uganda), Infectious Diseases Institute, Makerere University, Kampala

  • HEAL (2023 – present): team lead for a bilingual assistant that grounds GPT-4-turbo in Uganda's disease-surveillance guidelines, with a fine-tuned English–Luganda translation model. Supported by the Gates Foundation and Code for Africa. First author of the preprint.
  • Lacuna Luganda Health Corpus (funded by the Lacuna Fund): first author of the dataset paper, under review, describing 38,644 human-reviewed English–Luganda health sentence pairs released on Hugging Face.
  • AfriNorm (2026): lead developer of an open toolkit for restoring diacritics in African-language text, evaluated on six languages. First author of the paper draft.
  • Started two studies on Luganda machine translation (2026): how open models cope with speech that mixes Luganda and English (project lead), and predicting which machine translations need a human reviewer.
  • Built a model to estimate HIV risk among young people from behavioural data (SIDA Risk AI).
  • Developed a time-to-event model for the onset of cryptococcal meningitis in people living with HIV.
  • Built a retrieval-augmented question-answering system over long technical manuals.

2020 – present

Capacity Building Lead and Research Assistant Lecturer

ACE Uganda, Makerere University, Kampala

  • Developed and taught a Big Data Analytics course for MSc and PhD students.
  • Supervised student research projects.

2021 – 2023

Natural Language Specialist (contract)

Academy for Health Innovation, Infectious Diseases Institute, Kampala

  • Led the technical build of a clinical decision support system for health facilities.

2021 – 2023

Lead Data Scientist, COVID-19 response

Makerere University, Kampala

  • Technical lead for COAST, an English–Luganda COVID-19 chatbot that answers only with expert-approved responses. Built with Rasa and deployed on Kubernetes. Co-first author of the resulting paper in PLOS Digital Health (2026).
